# Modal verbs

Modal verbs sit before a main verb and express ability, permission, want, or obligation.
The main verb has no aspect marker in simple modal sentences.

| Modal | Pinyin | Core meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| can / able | `neng2` | physical ability, possibility, permission |
| can / know how | `hui4` | learned skill, likely future |
| may | `ke3yi3` | permission, possibility |
| want / need | `yao4` | desire, need, about to |
| would like | `xiang3` | wish, intention |
| must | `dei3` | obligation, necessity |
| should | `ying1gai1` | advice, expectation |
| be willing | `yuan4yi4` | willingness |

## Examples

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 neng2 qu4` | I can go |
| `wo3 hui4 shuo1 Zhong1wen2` | I can speak Chinese |
| `wo3 ke3yi3 jin4 lai2 ma5` | may I come in? |
| `wo3 yao4 yi1 bei1 shui3` | I want a glass of water |
| `wo3 xiang3 qu4 Zhong1guo2` | I would like to go to China |
| `wo3 dei3 zou3 le5` | I must go now |
| `ni3 ying1gai1 xiu1xi5` | you should rest |
| `ta1 bu2 yuan4yi4 qu4` | he is not willing to go |

## Negation

| Negative | Pinyin | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cannot | `bu4 neng2` | not able / not allowed |
| will not / cannot | `bu2 hui4` | will not happen / do not know how |
| may not | `bu4 ke3yi3` | not permitted |
| do not want | `bu4 xiang3` | not inclined |
| need not | `bu2 yong4` | no need |
| must not | `bie2` | do not (command) |

`bu2 yong4` replaces "need not" and takes a main verb: `ni3 bu2 yong4 lai2` (you do not
need to come).

## Distinguishing `neng2`, `hui4`, `ke3yi3`

- `neng2` is about capability or circumstances: `jin1tian1 wo3 bu4 neng2 qu4` (I cannot go
  today).
- `hui4` is about a learned skill or a likely future: `ta1 hui4 lai2` (he will come).
- `ke3yi3` is about permission: `zhe4li3 ke3yi3 zuo4` (you may sit here).

## Authoring notes

- Keep modals before the main verb with nothing between them.
- Choose the modal by meaning, not by the English word "can"; the three "can" forms above
  are not interchangeable.
- Negate with `bu4` (or `bu2 yong4`), never `mei2`, for modal meaning.
