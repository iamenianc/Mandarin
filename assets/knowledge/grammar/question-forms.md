# Question forms

Mandarin keeps statement word order in questions. The question is marked at the end, by a
verb repetition, or by putting a question word where the answer would go.

## Yes / no with `ma5`

Add `ma5` to the end of a statement. The word order does not change.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `ni3 shi4 Zhong1guo2 ren2 ma5` | are you Chinese? |
| `ni3 he1 cha2 ma5` | do you drink tea? |
| `ta1 you3 shou1ji1 ma5` | does he have a phone? |

Answers repeat the verb rather than saying "yes" or "no".

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `shi4` / `bu2 shi4` | yes / no (for `shi4`) |
| `you3` / `mei2you3` | have / do not have |
| `qu4` / `bu2 qu4` | going / not going |

## A-not-A

Repeat the verb or adjective with `bu4` between the two copies. This is common and needs
no particle.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `ni3 qu4 bu2 qu4` | are you going? |
| `ta1 mang2 bu4 mang2` | is he busy? |
| `ni3 you3 mei2 you3 qian2` | do you have money? |

For `you3`, the negative half is `mei2 you3`, not `bu2 you3`.

## Question words stay in place

Question words sit exactly where the answer would sit, with no fronting.

| Pinyin | English | Question word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ni3 jiao4 shen2me5 ming2zi5` | what is your name? | `shen2me5` what |
| `ta1 shi4 shei2` | who is he? | `shei2` who |
| `ni3 qu4 na3li3` | where are you going? | `na3li3` where |
| `ni3 shen2me5 shi2hou5 lai2` | when are you coming? | `shen2me5 shi2hou5` when |
| `zhe4 ge5 duo1shao5 qian2` | how much is this? | `duo1shao5` how much |
| `wei4 shen2me5` | why? | `wei4 shen2me5` why |

Use `ji3` when the answer is expected to be small (under ten) and `duo1shao5` for any
quantity.

## Follow-up with `ne5`

`ne5` asks "and ...?" after a related statement.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 hen3 hao3, ni3 ne5` | I am fine, and you? |
| `wo3 he1 cha2, ni3 ne5` | I am having tea, and you? |

## Choice with `hai2shi5`

`hai2shi5` offers a choice inside a question. In a statement, use `huo4zhe3` (or).

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `ni3 he1 cha2 hai2shi5 he1 ka1fei1` | tea or coffee? |
| `ni3 yao4 zhe4 ge5 hai2shi5 na4 ge5` | do you want this one or that one? |
| `wo3 xiang3 he1 cha2 huo4zhe3 ka1fei1` | I would like tea or coffee |

## Authoring notes

- Mark questions with `ma5`, A-not-A, or a question word only; do not also raise the whole
  sentence like English, which can sound surprised or rude.
- Keep question words in situ and make sure the expected answer slots into the same
  position.
- Never pair `ma5` with a question word; a question word already makes it a question.
