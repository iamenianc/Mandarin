# Measure words

Most nouns cannot be counted directly. A number or demonstrative is followed by a measure
word, then the noun: number + measure + noun.

## Common measure words

| Measure | Used for | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ge4` | general default; people | `yi1 ge4 ren2` | one person |
| `wei4` | people, politely | `liang3 wei4 xian1sheng5` | two gentlemen |
| `ben3` | books and bound items | `yi1 ben3 shu1` | one book |
| `zhang1` | flat objects: paper, tables, tickets | `yi1 zhang1 piao4` | one ticket |
| `tiao2` | long things: roads, rivers, fish, trousers | `yi1 tiao2 lu4` | one road |
| `jian1` | clothing, matters | `yi1 jian1 yi1fu5` | one garment |
| `bei1` | cups and glasses | `yi1 bei1 cha2` | one cup of tea |
| `wan3` | bowls | `yi1 wan3 fan4` | one bowl of rice |
| `ping2` | bottles | `yi1 ping2 shui3` | one bottle of water |
| `shuang1` | pairs | `yi1 shuang1 xie2` | one pair of shoes |
| `liang4` | vehicles | `yi1 liang4 che1` | one car |
| `zhi1` | animals, one of a pair | `yi1 zhi1 mao1` | one cat |
| `tou2` | large livestock | `yi1 tou2 niu2` | one cow |
| `ke1` | trees and plants | `yi1 ke1 shu4` | one tree |
| `duo3` | flowers and clouds | `yi1 duo3 hua1` | one flower |
| `jia1` | households and businesses | `yi1 jia1 fan4dian4` | one restaurant |
| `zuo4` | buildings and bridges | `yi1 zuo4 lou2` | one building |

## Two and a half rules

- **Use `liang3`, not `er4`, before a measure word.** `liang3 ge4 ren2` (two people), not
  `er4 ge4 ren2`. Use `er4` when counting, in ordinals, in dates, and in phone numbers.
- **Demonstratives take a measure word too.** `zhe4 ben3 shu1` (this book), `na4 ge5 ren2`
  (that person), `na3 wei4` (which person, polite).
- **Indefinite quantity uses `yi1dian3r` and `xie1`.** `yi1dian3r shui3` (a little water),
  `yi1xie1 dong1xi5` (some things).

## Counting without a special measure

When a noun already names a container or unit, that noun is the measure word:
`liang3 bei1`, `san1 ping2`, `si4 wan3`. Many time and money words also measure
themselves: `yi1 tian1`, `yi1 nian2`, `yi1 kuai4`.

## Authoring notes

- Every counted noun needs a measure word; a missing one is a common generated-item error.
- Default to `ge4` when unsure, but prefer the specific measure when it is common.
- Keep `liang3` before measures and `er4` in counting and dates.
- `men5` never combines with a number and measure word: `san1 ge5 xue2sheng5`, not
  `san1 ge5 xue2sheng5men5`.
