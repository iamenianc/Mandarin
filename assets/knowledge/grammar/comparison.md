# Comparison

Comparison uses a fixed frame rather than changing the adjective. `bi3` introduces the
standard being compared against.

## Superiority with `bi3`

A + `bi3` + B + adjective.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 bi3 ni3 gao1` | I am taller than you |
| `zhe4 ge5 bi3 na4 ge5 hao3` | this one is better than that one |
| `jin1tian1 bi3 zuo2tian1 re4` | today is hotter than yesterday |

Do not add `hen3` to the adjective in a `bi3` sentence.

## Equality and inferiority

| Pattern | Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A `gen1` B `yi1yang4` | `wo3 gen1 ni3 yi1yang4 gao1` | I am as tall as you |
| A `mei2you3` B + adj | `wo3 mei2you3 ni3 gao1` | I am not as tall as you |
| A `bu4ru2` B | `wo3 bu4ru2 ni3` | I am not as good as you |

`mei2you3` is the everyday way to say "less than"; `bu4ru2` also carries "not as good as".

## Negating a `bi3` sentence

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 bu4bi3 ni3 gao1` | I am not taller than you |
| `ta1 bu2 bi3 wo3 mang2` | he is not busier than I am |

## Degree

| Pinyin | English | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `geng4 gao1` | taller | `geng4` more |
| `zui4 gao1` | tallest | `zui4` most |
| `bi3jiao4 gui4` | relatively expensive | `bi3jiao4` relatively |
| `gao1 yi1dian3r` | a little taller | small difference |
| `gao1 de5 duo1` | much taller | large difference |

`zui4` does not need `bi3`: `ta1 zui4 gao1` (he is the tallest).

## Parallel change with `yue4 ... yue4`

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `yue4 kuai4 yue4 hao3` | the faster the better |
| `yue4 lai2 yue4 duo1` | more and more |

## Authoring notes

- Keep the compared quality as a bare adjective inside a `bi3` frame.
- Use `liang3` for "two" in comparison phrases with a measure word, as everywhere else.
- For beginners, prefer `bi3` and `mei2you3`; leave `bu4ru2` for later.
