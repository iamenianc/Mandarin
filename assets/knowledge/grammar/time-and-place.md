# Time and place

Time and place phrases come before the verb. Their internal order is fixed: larger units
before smaller ones, and location before the action.

## Time expressions

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `jin1tian1` | today |
| `ming2tian1` | tomorrow |
| `zuo2tian1` | yesterday |
| `xian4zai4` | now |
| `zao3shang5` | morning |
| `shang4wu3` | forenoon |
| `zhong1wu3` | noon |
| `xia4wu3` | afternoon |
| `wan3shang5` | evening |
| `yi3hou4` | later |
| `yi3qian2` | before |

Time goes after the subject, or before it for emphasis.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 ming2tian1 qu4` | I go tomorrow |
| `jin1tian1 wo3 hen3 mang2` | today I am busy |
| `wo3 xia4wu3 san1 dian3 jian4 ni3` | I will see you at three in the afternoon |

## Dates

Dates run from largest to smallest: year, month, day, then weekday.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `er4 ling2 er4 liu4 nian2` | the year 2026 |
| `yi1 yue4` | January |
| `san1 yue4 shi2 wu3 ri4` | the 15th of March |
| `xing1qi1 wu3` | Wednesday |

`hao4` is the spoken form for the day of the month: `shi2 wu3 hao4` (the 15th).

## Location with `zai4`

`zai4` + place comes before the verb and says where the action happens.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 zai4 jia1 chi1 fan4` | I eat at home |
| `ta1 zai4 yi1yuan4 gong1zuo4` | he works at the hospital |
| `wo3 zai4 zhe4li3 deng3 ni3` | I will wait here |

For a destination rather than a location, use `qu4` or `dao4` without `zai4`.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 qu4 yi1yuan4` | I am going to the hospital |
| `wo3 dao4 le5` | I have arrived |
| `ta1 qu4 le5 Beijing1` | he went to Beijing |

## From and to

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `cong2 Beijing1 dao4 Shang4hai3` | from Beijing to Shanghai |
| `cong2 zhe4li3 dao4 na4li3` | from here to there |

## Position words

Position words follow the noun they locate. Many take `mian4` or `bian1` in full form and
are often shortened.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `li3mian4` / `li3` | inside |
| `wai4mian4` / `wai4` | outside |
| `shang4mian4` / `shang4` | on top |
| `xia4mian4` / `xia4` | underneath |
| `qian2mian4` / `qian2` | in front |
| `hou4mian4` / `hou4` | behind |
| `pang2bian1` | beside |
| `dui4mian4` | opposite |
| `zhong1jian1` | between / middle |

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `zhuo1zi5 shang4` | on the table |
| `jia1 li3` | in the house |
| `yi1yuan4 pang2bian1` | beside the hospital |

## Duration

A duration follows the verb. With an object, the duration can come after the object or the
verb can be repeated.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 xue2 le5 san1 nian2 Zhong1wen2` | I studied Chinese for three years |
| `ta1 zhu4 le5 liang3 tian1` | he stayed for two days |
| `ni3 deng3 duo1jiu3 le5` | how long have you been waiting? |

## Authoring notes

- Keep time before the verb; English-style trailing time is the most common authoring slip.
- Use `zai4` for where an action happens and `qu4` / `dao4` for where it ends up.
- Keep dates in year-month-day order, and use `hao4` for spoken day numbers.
