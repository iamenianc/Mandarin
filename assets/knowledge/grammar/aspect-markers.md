# Aspect markers

Mandarin has no tense endings. Time is carried by time words, and aspect is carried by
particles after the verb: `le5` for completion, `guo5` for experience, and `zhe5` for an
ongoing state.

## `le5` - completed action or new situation

`le5` after the verb marks a completed action; `le5` at the end of the sentence marks a
change of state.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `ta1 mai3 le5 yi1 ben3 shu1` | he bought a book |
| `wo3 chi1 le5 fan4` | I have eaten |
| `ta1 lai2 le5` | he has come / he is here now |
| `xia4 yu3 le5` | it has started to rain |

Negate a completed action with `mei2` and drop `le5`:

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 mei2 chi1 fan4` | I have not eaten |
| `ta1 mei2 mai3 shu1` | he did not buy a book |

## `guo5` - experiential

`guo5` after the verb means the action has been experienced at least once.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 qu4 guo5 Zhong1guo2` | I have been to China |
| `wo3 chi1 guo5 jiao3zi5` | I have eaten dumplings |
| `ta1 mei2 kan4 guo5` | he has never seen it |

## `zhe5` - ongoing state

`zhe5` after the verb describes a state that continues.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `ta1 zuo4 zhe5` | he is sitting |
| `men2 kai1 zhe5` | the door is open |
| `ta1 xiao4 zhe5 shuo1` | he said, smiling |

## Progressive actions

For an action in progress, use `zheng4zai4` before the verb and often `ne5` at the end.
`zai4` alone also works.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 zheng4zai4 chi1 fan4 ne5` | I am eating right now |
| `ta1 zai4 da3 dian4hua4` | he is on the phone |
| `ni3 zai4 zuo4 shen2me5 ne5` | what are you doing? |

## Imminent change with `yao4 ... le5`

`yao4` before the verb with sentence-final `le5` marks something about to happen.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `kuai4 yao4 xia4 yu3 le5` | it is about to rain |
| `wo3 yao4 zou3 le5` | I am about to leave |

## Authoring notes

- Do not stack aspect markers: choose `le5`, `guo5`, or `zhe5` for the intended meaning.
- Keep time words explicit when the time frame is not obvious from context; aspect alone
  does not say when.
- When generating a completed-action item, check the negative form drops `le5`.
