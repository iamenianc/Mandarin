# Negation

Three negators cover almost everything: `bu4` for general negation, `mei2` for completion
and possession, and `bie2` for commands.

## `bu4` - general negation

Use `bu4` for present and future states, identity, ability, and habits. It goes directly
before the verb or adjective.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 bu2 shi4 lao3shi1` | I am not a teacher |
| `ta1 bu4 he1 ka1fei1` | he does not drink coffee |
| `wo3 bu2 qu4` | I am not going |
| `zhe4 ge5 bu2 gui4` | this is not expensive |

Before a fourth tone, `bu4` is spoken as second tone but still written `bu4`
(see `../pronunciation/tone-sandhi.md`).

| Pinyin | Spoken | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bu4 shi4` | bu2 shi4 | is not |
| `bu4 ke4 qi5` | bu2 ke4 qi5 | you're welcome |
| `bu4 yao4` | bu2 yao4 | do not want |

## `mei2` - completion and possession

Use `mei2` (often `mei2 you3`) for actions that did not happen, for events before now, and
for not having something. A completed verb with `le5` becomes negative with `mei2` and
drops `le5`.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 mei2 you3 qian2` | I do not have money |
| `ta1 mei2 lai2` | he did not come |
| `wo3 mei2 chi1 fan4` | I have not eaten |
| `ni3 mei2 qu4 guo5 ma5` | have you never been? |

Contrast the pair:

| Pinyin | English | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wo3 bu2 qu4` | I am not going | present intention |
| `wo3 mei2 qu4` | I did not go | past completion |
| `ta1 bu4 mang2` | he is not busy | present state |
| `ta1 mei2 kong4` | he was not free | past / availability |

## `bie2` - negative commands

Use `bie2` for "don't".

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `bie2 zou3` | do not go |
| `bie2 dan1xin1` | do not worry |
| `qing3 bie2 shuo1` | please do not say it |

## `bu4` in suggestions and questions

`bu4` also forms suggestion tags and polite checks.

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `ni3 bu2 qu4 ma5` | aren't you going? |
| `hao3 bu4 hao3` | is that all right? |
| `dui4 bu2 dui4` | right? |

## Authoring notes

- Choose `bu4` for states and intentions, `mei2` for completion and possession; mixing
  them is the most common grammar error in generated items.
- When negating a `le5` sentence, replace `le5` with `mei2` rather than keeping both.
- Keep `bie2` for instructions only; it does not negate statements.
