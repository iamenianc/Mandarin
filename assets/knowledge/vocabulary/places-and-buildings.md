## At a place

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `zai4 jia1` | at home |
| `qu4 xue2xiao4` | go to school |
| `zai4 gong1si1 shang4ban1` | work at the company |
| `dao4 le5 ji1chang3` | arrived at the airport |

## Authoring notes

- Use `zai4` for being at a place and `qu4` / `dao4` for going to one (see
  `../grammar/time-and-place.md`).
- Buildings take `zuo4` as a measure word: `yi1 zuo4 lou2`.
- Keep `ce4suo3` and `xi3shou3jian1` as a pair for asking after facilities.
