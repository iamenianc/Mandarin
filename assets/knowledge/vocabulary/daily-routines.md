## Example sentences

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 qi3chuang2 hen3 zao3` | I get up early |
| `wo3 shang4ban1, ran2hou4 xia4ban1` | I go to work, then leave work |
| `ni3 you3 kong4 ma5` | are you free? |
| `wo3 yao4 shui4jiao4 le5` | I am going to sleep |

## Authoring notes

- Keep time words with routines: `zao3shang5`, `xia4wu3`, `wan3shang5`.
- Verbs of routine pair well with `xian1 ... ran2hou4 ...` ordering.
- These items suit the daily loop because they can be rehearsed as one short exchange.
