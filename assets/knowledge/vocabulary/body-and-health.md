## Example sentences

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `wo3 tou2teng2` | I have a headache |
| `wo3 fa1shao1 le5` | I have a fever |
| `wo3 xu1yao4 yi1sheng1` | I need a doctor |
| `zhe4 ge5 yao4 yi1 tian1 chi1 san1 ci4` | take this medicine three times a day |

## Authoring notes

- Use `teng2` after the body part: `tou2 teng2`, `du4zi5 teng2`.
- Keep symptom phrases short, and pair them with the emergency theme.
- `xu1yao4` (to need) is useful for asking for care.
