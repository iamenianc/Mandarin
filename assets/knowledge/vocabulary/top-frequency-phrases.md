apologizing, asking for repetition, and handling money.

## Authoring notes

- These items are the natural first bundled set; keep them stable so early lessons do not
  churn.
- Pair each phrase with reference audio and a single target tone or segmental focus.
- Pronounce `bu2 ke4qi5` with the sandhi, but keep the written tone numbers as `bu4 ke4qi5`
  in content (see `../pronunciation/tone-sandhi.md`).
