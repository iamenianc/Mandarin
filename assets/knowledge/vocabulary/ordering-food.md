# Ordering food

Language for a restaurant or food stall: asking for the menu, ordering, adjusting
spiciness, and paying.

| Pinyin | English | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Example exchanges

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `qing3 gei3 wo3 cai4dan1` | please give me the menu |
| `wo3 yao4 yi1 wan3 mian4` | I would like a bowl of noodles |
| `bu2 yao4 la4, xie4xie5` | not spicy, thank you |
| `fu2wu4yuan2, mai3dan1` | server, the bill |
| `qing3 da3bao1` | please wrap it up to take away |

## Authoring notes

- Keep the container measure words (`bei1`, `wan3`, `ping2`) with their nouns.
- Spiciness is a frequent survival need; keep `bu2 yao4 la4` near the top of the theme.
- Use `mai3dan1` for casual speech and `jie2zhang4` for a more formal register.
