## Authoring notes

- Put the color after the noun with `de5`: `hong2 de5 yi1fu5` (a red garment).
- `lü4` keeps the umlaut because it follows `l`; after `j`, `q`, `x`, and `y` the same
  vowel is written `u` (see `../pronunciation/spelling-conventions.md`).
- Keep colors with the shopping theme for clothing and object drills.
