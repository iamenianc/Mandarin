## Authoring notes

- Keep `kan4` for all of see, watch, and read; context disambiguates.
- Distinguish `zhi1dao5` (know a fact) from `ren4shi5` (know a person).
- Use `xiang3` for a wish and `yao4` for a need or a firm want; see
  `../grammar/modal-verbs.md`.
