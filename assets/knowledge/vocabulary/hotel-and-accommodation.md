## At the front desk

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `you3 kong4fang2 ma5` | do you have rooms available? |
| `wo3 yu4ding4 le5 yi1 ge5 fang2jian1` | I reserved a room |
| `yi1 wan3 duo1shao5 qian2` | how much per night? |
| `bao1kuo4 zao3can1 ma5` | is breakfast included? |
| `fang2jian1 li3 you3 wang3luo4 ma5` | is there internet in the room? |
| `qing3 jiao4 xing3 wo3` | please give me a wake-up call |
| `wo3 yao4 tui4fang2` | I would like to check out |

## Authoring notes

- Use `yi1 wan3` for "per night" and pair with `duo1shao5 qian2`.
- Keep `ru4zhu4` and `tui4fang2` as the check-in and check-out pair.
- ID and passport are high-value survival vocabulary for travel.
