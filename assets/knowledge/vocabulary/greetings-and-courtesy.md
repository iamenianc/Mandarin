close an exchange.

## Authoring notes

- `ni3 hao3` is the default greeting; `nin2 hao3` suits a stranger or an elder.
- `qing3 wen4` is the polite way to open a question to a stranger.
- Keep the response pair `dui4bu5qi3` / `mei2guan1xi5` together in practice so the learner
  hears both halves.
