| --- | --- |

| `wo3 de5 shou1ji1 bu2 jian4 le5` | my phone is missing |

## Authoring notes

- Keep emergency phrases short and unembellished; they are for high-stress use.
- Include a spoken and a written-display form of key contacts, but no personal data.
- `ma2fan5` covers both "trouble" and "to inconvenience", useful as a general distress
  word.
