## Authoring notes

- Family terms distinguish older and younger siblings and paternal and maternal
  grandparents; keep the pairs together in drills.
- Address a stranger politely with `xian1sheng5` or `nü3shi4`, or with a title such as
  `lao3shi1` when it is known.
- `men5` pluralizes people words: `peng2you5men5` (friends).
