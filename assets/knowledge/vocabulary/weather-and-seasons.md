## Authoring notes

- Use `hui4` for likely future weather: `hui4 xia4 yu3` (it will rain).
- Keep temperature phrases simple: `san1 shi2 du4` (30 degrees).
- Weather is a natural early listening theme because the answers are short and concrete.
