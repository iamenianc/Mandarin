## Authoring notes

- `da3zhe2` means a percentage discount; state the price asked rather than a percentage in
  beginner drills.
- Keep `tai4 gui4 le5` as the polite opener for bargaining, with `pian2yi5 yi1dian3r` (a
  little cheaper) as the follow-up.
- Mobile payment vocabulary is common in daily life; `er4wei2ma3` is the everyday prompt.
