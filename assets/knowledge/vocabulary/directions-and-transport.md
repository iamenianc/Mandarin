## Asking the way

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `qing3 wen4, di4tie3zhan4 zai4 na3li3` | excuse me, where is the subway station? |
| `zen3me5 zou3` | how do I walk there? |
| `duo1yuan3` | how far? |
| `zuo3 zhuan3 jiu4 dao4 le5` | turn left and you are there |
| `zhe4 tiao2 lu4` | this road |

## Authoring notes

- Pair direction words with `zou3` and `zhuan3` in short imperatives.
- Keep `qing3 wen4` at the start of wayfinding questions.
- Transport words take `liang4` as the measure word: `yi1 liang4 chu1zu1che1`.
