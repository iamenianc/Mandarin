## Dates

| Pinyin | English |
| --- | --- |
| `nian2` | year |
| `qu4 nian2` | last year |
| `ming2 nian2` | next year |
| `yue4` | month |
| `yi1 yue4` | January |
| `shi2 er4 yue4` | December |
| `ri4` | day (written) |
| `hao4` | day (spoken) |
| `san1 yue4 shi2 wu3 hao4` | the 15th of March |

Dates run year, month, day, weekday: `er4 ling2 er4 liu4 nian2 san1 yue4 shi2 wu3 hao4`
(15 March 2026).

## Authoring notes

- Use `hao4` when speaking a date and `ri4` in written formal contexts.
- Keep time before the verb in sentences; see `../grammar/time-and-place.md`.
- `xing1qi1 tian1` is the everyday Sunday form.
