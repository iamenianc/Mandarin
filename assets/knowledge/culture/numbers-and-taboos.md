# Numbers and taboos

Numbers carry associations through sound, and those associations shape gifts, pricing,
and celebrations.

## Unlucky four

`si4` (four) sounds close to `si3` (death), so four is avoided where possible. Signs of
this: some buildings omit the fourth floor, and phone and gift amounts avoid four. Related
numbers such as `shi2 si4` (14) and `si4 shi2` (40) may also be avoided.

## Lucky eight

`ba1` (eight) sounds like `fa1` in `fa1 cai2` (to prosper), so eight is the most favored
number. Dates and amounts containing eight are considered auspicious, and `ba1 ba1` (88)
is a common choice.

## Other associations

| Number | Pinyin | Association |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `liu4` | six | smooth progress; `liu4 liu4 da4 shun4` wishes an easy course |
| `jiu3` | nine | lasting, from `jiu3` (a long time); good for weddings |
| `qi1` | seven | neutral in most settings, sometimes linked to the ghost month |
| `er4` | two | pairs, harmony; even numbers suit gifts |

## Colors and occasions

| Color | Pinyin | Occasion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| red | `hong2` | celebration, luck, weddings, Lunar New Year |
| gold | `jin1` | prosperity |
| white | `bai2` | mourning; avoid for gifts and celebrations |
| black | `hei1` | formal, but also mourning; avoid for gifts |

## Authoring notes

- Explain the sound link when teaching a taboo; the association is phonetic, not symbolic.
- Keep amounts in examples even and free of four when the context is a gift.
- These conventions are widespread but not universal; treat them as common usage rather
  than strict rules.
