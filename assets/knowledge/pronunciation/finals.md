# Finals

A final is everything after the initial: one or more vowels, optionally closed by a glide
or a nasal. The tone is carried on the final.

## Simple finals

| Final | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `a` | `ba1` | eight |
| `o` | `bo1` | wave |
| `e` | `he1` | to drink |
| `i` | `ni3` | you |
| `u` | `shu1` | book |
| `ü` | `nü3` | woman |
| `er` | `er2` | child |

`er` is the only final that can stand alone as a whole syllable with no initial, and it is
written with an `r` rather than a vowel letter.

## Nasal finals

| Final | Example | Meaning | Coda |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `an` | `san1` | three | front |
| `en` | `ren2` | person | front |
| `ang` | `mang2` | busy | back |
| `eng` | `leng3` | cold | back |
| `ong` | `dong1` | east | back |
| `ian` | `tian1` | sky / day | front |
| `in` | `xin1` | new | front |
| `iang` | `xiang1` | fragrant | back |
| `ing` | `xing1` | star | back |
| `iong` | `xiong2` | bear | back |
| `uan` | `chuan2` | boat | front |
| `un` | `cun1` | village | front |
| `uang` | `huang2` | yellow | back |
| `ueng` | `weng1` | old man | back |
| `üan` | `yuan2` | round | front |
| `ün` | `yun2` | cloud | front |

`ueng` only occurs with a zero initial, spelled `weng`; after an initial the related final
is `ong`.

## Palatal shorthand

After `j`, `q`, and `x`, the finals `üe`, `üan`, and `ün` are written `ue`, `uan`, and
`un`: `jue2` (to feel), `juan3` (to roll), `jun1` (army). The vowel is still `ü`. The
plain finals `ue`, `uan`, and `un` after other initials are separate sounds:
`guo2`, `guan1` (gate), `gun3` (to roll).

## Authoring notes

- Name finals by spelling in feedback: "the `-ng` final", "final `-ao`".
- The front / back nasal contrast (`an` / `ang`, `in` / `ing`, `en` / `eng`) is a common
  beginner problem; keep it in `minimal-pairs.md` drills.
- When a generated item uses a rare final, prefer a high-frequency example so the drill
  stays useful.
