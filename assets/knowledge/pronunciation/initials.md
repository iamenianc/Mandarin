# Initials

Mandarin has 21 consonant initials. A syllable may also have no initial, in which case it
is spelled with `y` or `w` rather than an empty onset.

| Initial | Type | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `b` | unaspirated labial | `ba1` | eight |
| `p` | aspirated labial | `pa2` | to climb |
| `m` | nasal labial | `ma1` | mother |
| `f` | fricative labial | `fan4` | rice / meal |
| `d` | unaspirated alveolar | `da4` | big |
| `t` | aspirated alveolar | `tian1` | sky / day |
| `n` | nasal alveolar | `ni3` | you |
| `l` | lateral alveolar | `lai2` | to come |
| `g` | unaspirated velar | `ge1` | elder brother |
| `k` | aspirated velar | `kan4` | to look |
| `h` | fricative velar | `hao3` | good |
| `j` | unaspirated palatal | `jiu3` | nine |
| `q` | aspirated palatal | `qi1` | seven |
| `x` | fricative palatal | `xie4` | thanks |
| `zh` | unaspirated retroflex | `zhong1` | middle |
| `ch` | aspirated retroflex | `chi1` | to eat |
| `sh` | fricative retroflex | `shi2` | ten |
| `r` | retroflex approximant | `ren2` | person |
| `z` | unaspirated dental | `zou3` | to walk |
| `c` | aspirated dental | `cai4` | vegetable / dish |
| `s` | fricative dental | `san1` | three |

## Contrasts to teach first

- **Aspiration.** `b` / `p`, `d` / `t`, `g` / `k`, `j` / `q`, `zh` / `ch`, `z` / `c` are
  the same mouth position with and without a puff of air. The puff changes the word:
  `ba1` (eight) versus `pa2` (climb).
- **Retroflex versus dental.** `zh`, `ch`, `sh` curl the tongue tip back; `z`, `c`, `s`
  keep it forward. Compare `shi2` (ten) and `si4` (four).
- **Palatals.** `j`, `q`, `x` are made with the tongue high and forward; they only combine
  with `i` and `ü` finals, and `ü` is written `u` after them (`ju4`, `que4`, `xu1`).

## Zero initial

Syllables that begin with a vowel use `y` or `w` in spelling: `i` -> `yi`, `ie` -> `ye`,
`u` -> `wu`, `uo` -> `wo`, `ü` -> `yu`. There is no consonant sound added; the spelling is
purely orthographic. See `spelling-conventions.md` for the full mapping.

## Authoring notes

- Name initials by their letters in feedback: "the `x` initial", "the `zh` initial".
- Keep aspiration and retroflex contrasts in minimal-pair drills; they are the most
  common beginner confusions with English-based expectations.
