# Minimal pairs

Minimal pairs isolate one contrast at a time. They are the raw material for listening
discrimination and production drills in the tones and fundamentals modules.

## Tone contrasts

Same syllable, different tone, different meaning. Hearing and naming the tone is the
core tones-module exercise.

| Pinyin | Meaning | Contrast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ma1` | mother | high level |
| `ma2` | hemp | rising |
| `ma3` | horse | low dipping |
| `ma4` | scold | falling |
| `ma5` | question particle | neutral |

## Authoring notes

- Give one contrast per drill item so feedback can name the single feature that was wrong.
- Pair each item with reference audio; tone drills in particular depend on hearing the
  contour, not reading a description.
- Keep the English meaning in the item so the drill is about sound, not translation.
