# Greeting and introduction

Two people meet and exchange names. Uses the courtesy phrases in
`greetings-courtesy.md`.

| speaker | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | `ni3 hao3!` | hello! |
| B | `ni3 hao3!` | hello! |
| A | `wo3 xing4 Wang2. ni3 ne5?` | my surname is Wang. and you? |
| B | `wo3 xing4 Li3.` | my surname is Li. |
| A | `hen3 gao1 xing4 ren4 shi5 ni3.` | nice to meet you. |
| B | `wo3 ye3 shi4.` | me too. |

`ni3 ne5` returns the same question to the other person; `ne5` is neutral in this
use.
