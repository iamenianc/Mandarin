# Greetings and courtesy

Greeting, thanks, apology, and polite-opener phrases for first contact. Part of
the beginner and survival themes in ADR 0008.

| pinyin | meaning | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ni3 hao3` | hello | General greeting at any time of day. |
| `nin2 hao3` | hello (polite) | Use with a stranger, an elder, or anyone owed respect. |
| `zao3 shang5 hao3` | good morning | Morning greeting. |
| `wan3 shang5 hao3` | good evening | Evening greeting. |
| `wan3 an1` | good night | Said on parting at night. |
| `ni3 hao3 ma5` | how are you? | Greeting question; the answer is often the same phrase. |
| `wo3 hen3 hao3` | I am fine | Standard answer to `ni3 hao3 ma5`. |
| `xie4 xie5` | thank you | The second syllable is neutral. |
| `bu2 ke4 qi5` | you are welcome | `bu4` is spoken as `bu2` before a fourth tone. |
| `dui4 bu5 qi3` | sorry | Apology; also used to get someone's attention. |
| `mei2 guan1 xi5` | it is okay | Reassurance or reply to an apology. |
| `bu4 hao3 yi4 si5` | excuse me | Soft apology or polite interruption. |
| `qing3 wen4` | may I ask | Opener before asking for information. |
| `qing3` | please | Polite marker before a request. |
| `hen3 gao1 xing4 ren4 shi5 ni3` | nice to meet you | Said after an introduction. |
| `zai4 jian4` | goodbye | Standard farewell. |
| `huan1 ying2` | welcome | Greeting to a guest. |
