# Tone pairs

Two-syllable examples that show how tones meet. The written tone and the spoken
tone can differ, and tone numbers record what is spoken (ADR 0012).

| written | spoken | meaning | note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ni3 hao3` | `ni2 hao3` | hello | A third tone before another third tone is spoken as a second tone. |
| `bu4 ke4 qi5` | `bu2 ke4 qi5` | you are welcome | `bu4` is spoken as `bu2` before a fourth tone. |
| `bu4 yao4` | `bu2 yao4` | do not want | The same `bu4` change before a fourth tone. |
| `yi1 tian1` | `yi4 tian1` | one day | `yi1` is spoken as `yi4` before a first tone. |
| `yi1 nian2` | `yi4 nian2` | one year | `yi1` is spoken as `yi4` before a second tone. |
| `yi1 ben3` | `yi4 ben3` | one book | `yi1` is spoken as `yi4` before a third tone. |
| `yi1 ge5` | `yi2 ge5` | one (of something) | `yi1` is spoken as `yi2` before a fourth tone. |
| `ma1 ma5` | `ma1 ma5` | mother | A repeated syllable often takes the neutral tone. |
| `xie4 xie5` | `xie4 xie5` | thank you | Fourth tone plus neutral tone. |
| `peng2 you5` | `peng2 you5` | friend | A third tone reduced to neutral. |
| `dong1 xi5` | `dong1 xi5` | thing | A first tone plus neutral tone. |
| `zai4 jian4` | `zai4 jian4` | goodbye | Fourth tone plus fourth tone. |
