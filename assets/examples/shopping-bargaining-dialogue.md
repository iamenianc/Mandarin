# Bargaining at a market stall

A shopper asks the price and agrees a lower one. Uses the phrases in
`shopping-prices.md`.

| speaker | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopper | `qing3 wen4, zhe4 ge5 duo1 shao5 qian2?` | excuse me, how much is this? |
| Seller | `wu3 shi2 kuai4.` | fifty yuan. |
| Shopper | `tai4 gui4 le5! si4 shi2 kuai4 xing2 ma5?` | too expensive! how about forty? |
| Seller | `si4 shi2 wu3.` | forty-five. |
| Shopper | `hao3, jiu4 si4 shi2 wu3.` | all right, forty-five then. |
| Seller | `xie4 xie5.` | thank you. |
