# Tone contours

The four tones and the neutral tone, each shown with the same syllable so the
contour is the only thing that changes (ADR 0012). The contour column uses the
Chao five-point scale.

| tone | number | contour | example | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| first | 1 | 55 | `ma1` | mother |
| second | 2 | 35 | `ma2` | hemp |
| third | 3 | 214 | `ma3` | horse |
| fourth | 4 | 51 | `ma4` | scold |
| neutral | 5 | - | `ma5` | question particle |

`ma1` is high and level, `ma2` rises, `ma3` dips and rises, and `ma4` falls
sharply. The neutral tone is short and light, and its pitch follows the tone
before it. The app names tones by number, matching what appears on screen.

The same contours are drawn in `fundamentals-tone-contours.svg`, a hand-authored
diagram that uses no external assets.
