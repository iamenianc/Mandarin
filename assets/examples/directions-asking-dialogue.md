# Asking for directions

A visitor asks the way and a passer-by answers. Uses the phrases in
`directions-transport.md`.

| speaker | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor | `qing3 wen4, huo3 che1 zhan4 zai4 na3 li3?` | excuse me, where is the train station? |
| Local | `yi4 zhi2 zou3, ran2 hou4 wang3 you4 zhuan3.` | go straight, then turn right. |
| Visitor | `li2 zhe4 li3 yuan3 ma5?` | is it far from here? |
| Local | `bu2 tai4 yuan3, zou3 shi2 fen1 zhong1.` | not too far, a ten-minute walk. |
| Visitor | `xie4 xie5.` | thank you. |
| Local | `bu2 ke4 qi5.` | you are welcome. |
