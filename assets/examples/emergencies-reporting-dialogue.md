# Reporting a lost wallet

A visitor asks for help and a passer-by takes them to the police. Uses the
phrases in `emergencies-help.md`.

| speaker | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor | `jiu4 ming4! wo3 de5 qian2 bao1 diu1 le5!` | help! I lost my wallet! |
| Local | `bie2 ji2, man4 man5 shuo1.` | do not worry, speak slowly. |
| Visitor | `wo3 zai4 di4 tie3 zhan4 diu1 le5 qian2 bao1.` | I lost my wallet at the subway station. |
| Local | `qing3 gen1 wo3 lai2. wo3 men5 qu4 jing3 cha2 ju2.` | please come with me. we will go to the police station. |
| Visitor | `hao3, xie4 xie5.` | all right, thank you. |
| Local | `bu2 ke4 qi5.` | you are welcome. |
