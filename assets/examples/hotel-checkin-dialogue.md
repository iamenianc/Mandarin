# Checking in at a hotel

A guest checks in and receives a room card. Uses the phrases in `hotel-stays.md`.

| speaker | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest | `nin2 hao3, wo3 you3 yu4 ding4.` | hello, I have a reservation. |
| Clerk | `nin2 hao3. qing3 wen4 nin2 xing4 shen2 me5?` | hello. what is your surname? |
| Guest | `wo3 xing4 Wang2.` | my surname is Wang. |
| Clerk | `hao3 de5. yi2 ge5 fang2 jian1, san1 wan3.` | all right. one room, three nights. |
| Guest | `dui4, san1 wan3.` | yes, three nights. |
| Clerk | `qing3 chu1 shi4 nin2 de5 hu4 zhao4.` | please show your passport. |
| Guest | `gei3 nin2.` | here you are. |
| Clerk | `zhe4 shi4 nin2 de5 fang2 ka3, san1 ling2 san1 hao4 fang2.` | this is your room card, room 303. |
