Greetings are the first words taught after the tones. Each is heard as a whole phrase
before its pinyin and meaning are shown, and each is short enough to repeat back
immediately.

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ni3 hao3 | hello |
| 2 | ni3 hao3 ma5 | how are you |
| 3 | wo3 hen3 hao3 | I am fine |
| 4 | ni3 ne5 | and you |
| 5 | zai4 jian4 | goodbye |
| 6 | zao3 shang5 hao3 | good morning |
| 7 | wan3 shang5 hao3 | good evening |

## Notes

- `ni3 hao3` contains two third tones; the first is spoken as tone 2, not written
  that way (`../../beginner/fundamentals/fundamentals-05-tone-sandhi.md`).
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
