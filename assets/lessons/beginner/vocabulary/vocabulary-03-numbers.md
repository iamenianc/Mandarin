Numbers are taught from zero to ten as isolated syllables, so each tone is heard
clearly. Combinations are introduced later, because the tone of `yi1` changes in
context (`fundamentals-05-tone-sandhi.md`).

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |

| 2 | yi1 | one |
| 3 | er4 | two |

| 5 | si4 | four |
| 6 | wu3 | five |
| 7 | liu4 | six |
| 8 | qi1 | seven |
| 9 | ba1 | eight |
| 10 | jiu3 | nine |
| 11 | shi2 | ten |
| 12 | liang3 | two (before a measure word) |

## Notes

- `er4` and `liang3` both mean two; `liang3` is used with a measure word.
- Counting numbers in isolation keeps the dictionary tone; combinations can change
  (`fundamentals-05-tone-sandhi.md`).
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
