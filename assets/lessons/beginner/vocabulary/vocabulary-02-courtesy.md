Courtesy phrases keep an exchange going and buy time when the learner is unsure.
Each item is a complete spoken unit, so it can be replayed and repeated without any
assembly.

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | qing3 | please |
| 2 | qing3 wen4 | excuse me |
| 3 | xie4 xie5 | thank you |
| 4 | dui4 bu5 qi3 | sorry |
| 5 | mei2 guan1 xi5 | it is okay |
| 6 | huan1 ying2 | welcome |

## Notes

- `qing3 wen4` is used to open a question, not only to apologise.
- `dui4 bu5 qi3` carries a neutral tone in the middle syllable.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
