Time words and the clock are the last beginner theme before travel phrases. Each item
is heard in a short frame, such as asking the time, rather than as a list entry.

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | xian4 zai4 | now |
| 2 | jin1 tian1 | today |
| 3 | ming2 tian1 | tomorrow |
| 4 | zuo2 tian1 | yesterday |
| 5 | zao3 shang5 | morning |
| 6 | wan3 shang5 | evening |
| 7 | dian3 | o'clock |
| 8 | fen1 | minute |
| 9 | ji3 dian3 le5 | what time is it |

## Notes

- `ji3 dian3 le5` is a complete question and is practised as one unit.
- O'clock is spoken as a number plus `dian3`, for example `san1 dian3` for three
  o'clock.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
