# First tone

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| id | tones-01-first-tone |
| module | tones |
| lesson | 01 |
| level | beginner |
| topic | tone contours |

The first tone is high and level. It is held on a steady pitch without rising or
falling, and it is written with tone number 1 after the syllable. The learner hears
the contour first, then names it.

## Items

| # | pinyin | contour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ma1 | high and level |
| 2 | ba1 | high and level |
| 3 | ta1 | high and level |

## Notes

- `ma1` to `ma5` carry the same syllable across the five tones (ADR 0012).
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
