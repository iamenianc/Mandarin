# Fourth tone

The fourth tone falls. It starts high and drops sharply, and it is written with tone
number 4. The learner hears the fall before it is named.

## Items

| # | pinyin | contour |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | shi4 | falling |

## Notes

- The fourth tone is short; the drop is carried by pitch, not by volume.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
