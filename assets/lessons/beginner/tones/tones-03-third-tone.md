# Third tone

The third tone dips. It falls and then rises again within the syllable, and it is
written with tone number 3. In connected speech the full dip is often reduced; the
lesson teaches the full contour before the reduction.

## Items

| # | pinyin | contour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ma3 | dipping |
| 2 | ni3 | dipping |
| 3 | hao3 | dipping |

## Notes

- Two third tones in sequence change the first one; that is tone sandhi
  (`../fundamentals/fundamentals-05-tone-sandhi.md`).
