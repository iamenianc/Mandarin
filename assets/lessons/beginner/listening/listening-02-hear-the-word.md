# Hear the word

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| id | listening-02-hear-the-word |
| module | listening |
| lesson | 02 |
| level | beginner |
| topic | word identification |

A whole word or phrase is played and the learner chooses the matching pinyin or
meaning. The lesson builds on the tone drills by asking for the word as well as the
sound, still without speaking.

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ni3 hao3 | hello |
| 2 | xie4 xie5 | thank you |
| 3 | zai4 jian4 | goodbye |
| 4 | dui4 bu5 qi3 | sorry |

## Notes

- Choices are shown as pinyin with tone numbers, or as English meanings.
- Timing is untimed by default, so the learner can replay and think.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
