# Hear the tone

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| id | listening-01-hear-the-tone |
| module | listening |
| lesson | 01 |
| level | beginner |
| topic | tone identification |

One tone carrier is played at a time and the learner names the tone number on a large
control. The lesson teaches the loop before it becomes a timed drill; there is no
speaking and no scoring (ADR 0005).

## Items

| # | pinyin | correct response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ma1 | 1 |
| 2 | ma2 | 2 |
| 3 | ma3 | 3 |
| 4 | ma4 | 4 |
| 5 | ma5 | 5 |

## Notes

- The same syllable across the five tones keeps the choice about the contour alone.
- The clip can be replayed at any time; feedback is immediate and names the tone
  number.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
