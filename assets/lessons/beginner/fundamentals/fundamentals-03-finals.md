The final carries the vowel sound of a syllable. Some finals are a single vowel, and
others are combinations that glide from one vowel to another or end in a nasal. Each
is heard inside a syllable, with its tone number written after it.

## Notes

- `ü` is written as `u` after `j`, `q`, `x`, and `y`, as in `yu2`.
- Finals can combine with a medial glide, as in `jia1` and `shui3`.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
