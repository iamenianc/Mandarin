# Tone sandhi

| Field | Value |

Tones are not always spoken as they are written. Three common changes are heard in
beginner speech: two third tones in a row, and the tones of `bu4` and `yi1` before
another syllable. The lesson writes the underlying form and the spoken form side by
side so the learner can hear the difference.

## Items

| # | pinyin (written) | pinyin (spoken) | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ni3 hao3 | ni2 hao3 | hello |
| 2 | bu4 shi4 | bu2 shi4 | is not |
| 3 | yi1 ban4 | yi2 ban4 | one half |
| 4 | yi1 tian1 | yi4 tian1 | one day |
| 5 | ting1 bu5 dong3 | ting1 bu5 dong3 | do not understand by hearing |

## Notes

- Two third tones: the first is spoken as tone 2.
- `bu4` before a fourth tone is spoken as tone 2; in some words it is neutral tone 5.
- `yi1` before a fourth tone is spoken as tone 2; before tones 1, 2, and 3 it is
  spoken as tone 4; counted alone or in ordinals it stays tone 1.
- The written form keeps the dictionary tone; feedback names the spoken tone.
