The initials are the consonants that can open a syllable. There are twenty-one of
them, and each is practised inside a whole syllable so the sound is heard in context
rather than in isolation.

## Notes

- `j`, `q`, and `x` combine only with `i` and `ü` finals.
- `zh`, `ch`, `sh`, and `r` are retroflex; `z`, `c`, and `s` are not.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
