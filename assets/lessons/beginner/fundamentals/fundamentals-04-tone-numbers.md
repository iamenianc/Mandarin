# Tone numbers

| Field | Value |

Every syllable is written with its tone as a number, from 1 to 5, placed after the
final. Tone marks are not used in the app; the number is the notation the learner
sees in lessons, feedback, and progress (ADR 0012). Numbering the tones also makes
them easy to quote when feedback names a syllable.

## Items

| # | pinyin | tone | contour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ma1 | 1 | high and level |
| 2 | ma2 | 2 | rising |
| 3 | ma3 | 3 | dipping |
| 4 | ma4 | 4 | falling |
| 5 | ma5 | 5 | neutral |

## Notes

- The same syllable `ma` across five tones shows the notation without changing any
  other sound.
- Words carry one number per syllable: `ni3 hao3`, `xie4 xie5`.
- Feedback quotes the same numbers, so coaching maps back to the pinyin on screen
  (ADR 0005, ADR 0012).
