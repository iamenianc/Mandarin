A Mandarin syllable has an initial, a final, and a tone. The initial is the consonant
that opens the syllable and can be absent; the final carries the vowel sound; the tone
is written as a number after the final. Hearing the parts separately makes the whole
syllable easier to repeat.

## Notes

- `er4` has no initial; a syllable can begin directly on its final.
- The initial is a sound, not a letter name; it is always heard with its final.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
