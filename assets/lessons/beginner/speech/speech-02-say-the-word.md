# Say the word

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| id | speech-02-say-the-word |
| module | speech |
| lesson | 02 |
| level | beginner |
| topic | word production |

A whole word or phrase is played and the learner repeats it. Feedback names the
weakest syllable and tone number, so coaching maps back to the pinyin on screen
(ADR 0005). The lesson uses phrases already taught in the vocabulary module.

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ni3 hao3 | hello |
| 2 | xie4 xie5 | thank you |
| 3 | wo3 hen3 hao3 | I am fine |
| 4 | zai4 jian4 | goodbye |

## Notes

- `ni3 hao3` contains two third tones; feedback refers to the spoken tone, not only
  the written one (`../../beginner/fundamentals/fundamentals-05-tone-sandhi.md`).
- The learner can retry or move on; nothing is marked wrong.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
