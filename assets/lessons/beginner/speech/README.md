# Speech

The speech module lessons: say-and-repeat teaching before the reps move to practice.
The learner hears a reference, repeats it, and receives soft feedback that names the
weakest sound by pinyin syllable and tone number (ADR 0005). The lessons run the full
loop once so the learner knows what to expect.

## Sequence

| Lesson | Title | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `speech-01-say-the-tone.md` | Say the tone | Produce one tone at a time |
| `speech-02-say-the-word.md` | Say the word | Produce a whole word or phrase |

Feedback is encouraging and approximate; it never shows a grade (ADR 0005). Reference
audio is bundled and generated at build time (ADR 0006).
