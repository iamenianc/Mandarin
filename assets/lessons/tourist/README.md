# Tourist

Travel Mandarin for everyday needs: ordering food, directions, transport, hotel, and
shopping (ADR 0008). Lessons stay audio-first; pinyin is always shown with tone
numbers, and no hanzi is displayed.

## Modules

| Folder | Module | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `vocabulary/` | vocabulary | Travel themes: food, directions, transport, hotel, shopping |

Listening and speech practice reuse the items taught here; the lessons add no reps of
their own. Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin (ADR 0006).
