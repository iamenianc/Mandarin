# Directions

Directions are taught as a question and the words needed to act on the answer. The
learner first hears how to ask where something is, then the short direction words and
place names that are likely in the reply.

## Notes

- A place plus `zai4 na3 li3` forms the question, for example
  `xi3 shou3 jian1 zai4 na3 li3`.
- `wang3 qian2 zou3` is a direction to follow, not a question.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
