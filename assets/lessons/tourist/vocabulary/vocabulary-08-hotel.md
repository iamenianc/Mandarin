Hotel items follow the front-desk exchange: booking, arriving, and asking about the
room. The learner hears each phrase as a complete unit, then repeats it.

## Notes

- `yi1 wan3` pairs a number with a night; the tone of `yi1` can change in context
  (`../../beginner/fundamentals/fundamentals-05-tone-sandhi.md`).
- `re4 shui3` is a common request; both syllables carry full tones.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
