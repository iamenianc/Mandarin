# Ordering food

Ordering food is the first tourist theme. Each item is heard in the order a learner
would use it: ask, choose, and settle the bill. Whole phrases are taught alongside the
single words they contain.

## Notes

- `wo3 yao4` plus an item is enough to order, for example `wo3 yao4 cha2`.
- `zhe4 ge5` is the standard way to point at one item on a menu.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
