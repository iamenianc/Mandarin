# Transport

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |

| module | vocabulary |
| lesson | 07 |

Transport words cover choosing a vehicle, buying a ticket, and getting on and off.
Each is taught in a short frame so the learner can hear it in a real exchange rather
than as a label.

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | chu1 zu1 che1 | taxi |
| 2 | gong1 jiao1 che1 | bus |
| 3 | che1 zhan4 | station, stop |
| 4 | piao4 | ticket |
| 5 | shang4 che1 | get on |
| 6 | xia4 che1 | get off |
| 7 | dao4 | to, arrive |
| 8 | duo1 shao5 qian2 | how much money |

## Notes

- A destination plus `dao4` names where the learner is going.
- `duo1 shao5 qian2` is the standard fare question and is reused in the shopping
  lesson.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
