# Shopping

Shopping closes the tourist level with the vocabulary of price, payment, and
preference. The lesson pairs a phrase with the situation it belongs to, such as
asking a price or declining one.

## Notes

- `mai3` and `mai4` differ only by tone; the pair is a useful tone contrast.
- `duo1 shao5` drops `qian2` when the price is already the topic.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
