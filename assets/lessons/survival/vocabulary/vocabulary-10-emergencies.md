Emergency items are the smallest set that gets a learner help. Each is short, and
several are single words that can be said without any sentence frame.

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | jiu4 ming4 | help |
| 2 | bang1 bang1 wo3 | help me |
| 3 | wo3 mi2 lu4 le5 | I am lost |
| 4 | yi1 yuan4 | hospital |
| 5 | jiu4 hu4 che1 | ambulance |
| 6 | jing3 cha2 | police |
| 7 | huo3 jing3 | fire |
| 8 | da3 dian4 hua4 | make a phone call |
| 9 | hu4 zhao4 | passport |
| 10 | diu1 le5 | lost, misplaced |

## Notes

- `jiu4 ming4` is the shortest call for help and is practised as one unit.
- `diu1 le5` describes something lost; `wo3 mi2 lu4 le5` describes being lost.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
