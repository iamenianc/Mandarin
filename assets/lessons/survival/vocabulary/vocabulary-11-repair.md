# Repair phrases

Repair phrases keep a conversation alive when the learner is not understanding. They
are taught last because they depend on the learner already recognising that something
was missed, and they are the phrases that buy another attempt.

## Items

| # | pinyin | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | wo3 bu4 dong3 | I do not understand |
| 2 | ting1 bu5 dong3 | do not understand by hearing |
| 3 | ting1 bu5 qing1 chu5 | cannot hear clearly |
| 4 | qing3 chong2 fu4 | please repeat |
| 5 | qing3 shuo1 | please speak |
| 6 | man4 man4 shuo1 | speak slowly |
| 7 | qing3 bang1 wo3 | please help me |

## Notes

- `wo3 bu4 dong3` states the problem; `ting1 bu5 dong3` names hearing as the cause.
- The neutral tone in `ting1 bu5 dong3` and `ting1 bu5 qing1 chu5` is part of the
  pattern, not a reduction the learner adds.
- Reference audio is generated at build time from the pinyin above and bundled under
  `assets/audio/` (ADR 0006).
