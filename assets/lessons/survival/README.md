# Survival

Mandarin for getting help and being understood: emergency and repair phrases for a
learner who cannot fall back on reading (ADR 0008). The level keeps to short,
high-priority phrases that can be replayed and repeated under pressure.

## Modules

| Folder | Module | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `vocabulary/` | vocabulary | Emergencies and repair phrases |

Lessons stay audio-first; pinyin is always shown with tone numbers, and no hanzi is
displayed. Reference audio is bundled so the phrases work offline (ADR 0006,
ADR 0010).
